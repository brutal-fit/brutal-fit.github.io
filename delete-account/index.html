Delete your account

Effective date: [effective date]

You can delete your BRUTAL account at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

On this page

  1. Delete from inside the app
  2. Delete by email
  3. What is deleted
  4. What we may keep, and for how long
  5. Export first

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the BRUTAL app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your request is recorded immediately and your account is closed.

Delete by email

If you cannot sign in, email [privacy contact email] from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify that the request comes from you and then delete the account.

What is deleted

Your profile, preferences, progress entries, messages, notifications, device activity and waitlist entry are deleted or anonymised within 30 days.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Booking records, reviews, support cases and reports, in anonymised form, as long as needed to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.
  • Trainer verification documents, for the period the law requires after a trainer leaves the platform.
  • Server access logs kept by our hosting provider for security, for a limited period.

Export first

If you want a copy of your data before deleting, use Settings, Privacy, Request data export in the app, or email [privacy contact email].

See our privacy policy for the full picture. Questions: [privacy contact email].
