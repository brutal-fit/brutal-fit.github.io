The basics

How it works

From a goal to your first session in three steps.

  1. Tell us your goal

    Strength, fat loss, mobility, a sport, or just showing up. Pick your experience level and how you like to train.

  2. Choose a verified trainer near you

    Every trainer on BRUTAL has been through our verification. Profiles show a service area, specialities and availability.

  3. Book a slot. Train at home or online.

    Pick a day and time that works. The trainer comes to you, or you meet online. Reschedule or cancel under a policy you can read before you book.

For clients

Built for how you actually train

  • Verified trainers

    Documents checked and experience reviewed before anyone can be booked.

  • Sessions at home

    Trainers who travel to your area. Your exact address is shared only after the booking is confirmed.

  • Online programmes

    Structured plans with a trainer behind them. Today's session, your week, your assessment, in one place.

  • Progress that is yours

    Log weight and measurements, see the trend. Your progress entries are visible only to you.

  • Messages and reminders

    Talk to your trainer in the app. Session reminders and booking updates come to you, not your inbox.

  • Roadmap

    AI coach, on the roadmap

    A coach in your pocket between sessions. We are choosing the provider carefully; it ships when it is good.

For trainers

For trainers who want clients, not admin

You are good at training people. You should not have to be good at marketing, chasing payments and guarding your address. BRUTAL brings you clients who are ready to train, handles booking and rescheduling, and sets a fair platform price so you never negotiate.

Apply as a trainer
  • Apply in the app with your documents and experience. We review, you get a decision.
  • Set your weekly availability and time off. Clients book only inside it.
  • Accept or decline requests. Run sessions at the client's place or online.
  • See your clients, keep private notes, track earnings.

The rate you submit is your expectation. BRUTAL reviews it and sets the platform price clients see.

Safety

Safety and trust, by design

What BRUTAL does to keep clients and trainers safe.

  • Service area, never an address

    Trainer profiles show the area they cover. A home address is never public.

  • Addresses unlock on confirmation

    A session address is visible only to the client and trainer, and only once the booking is confirmed.

  • Verification before visibility

    Trainers are not listed until BRUTAL has reviewed their documents.

  • Report and support, in-app

    Report a session, a message or a review from inside the app. Support cases are private to you and BRUTAL.

Pricing

Pricing you can read

  • Set by BRUTAL

    BRUTAL sets the price, so the price you see is the price everyone sees.

  • Locked at booking

    The price is locked when your booking is confirmed. A later change never touches it.

  • No invented discounts

    If we show a saving, it is against the real base rate.

Payments are not live yet. The app's checkout is a clearly marked test until our payment partner is in place.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Where is BRUTAL available?

We are launching in India, Android first. Join the waitlist and tell us your city; it helps us decide where to open first.

How are trainers verified?

Trainers apply in the app with identity and qualification documents and their experience. BRUTAL reviews every application before a trainer can be listed or booked.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Trainers list the area they cover. Your exact address is shared with your trainer only after the booking is confirmed.

Who decides the price?

BRUTAL does. A trainer submits the rate they expect, we review it and set the platform price you see. That price is locked when your booking is confirmed.

What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?

You can do both from the booking. The policy is shown before you book and again when you change a booking.

Is online training available?

Online programmes are part of the app. Real payments are not switched on yet, so programme purchases are a marked test until launch.

I am a trainer. How do I join?

Join the waitlist as a trainer. When we open in your area, you will apply in the app with your documents and set your availability.
