1. Who this policy covers

  • Clients: people who use the App to find, book and train with trainers.
  • Trainers: people who apply to offer training through the App.
  • Visitors: people who browse the Site or join the waitlist.

2. Data we collect and why

We collect only what the App needs to work. The table lists each category, where it comes from and why we use it.

Category What it includes Why we use it
Account Email address, password (stored only as a hash), a verification code sent to your email, your role (client or trainer), display name, username, city, optional profile photo To create and secure your account, sign you in and show your profile to the people you train with
Client preferences Training goals, experience level, how you like to train To match you with suitable trainers and personalise the App
Trainer application Name, username, city, experience, services offered, expected rate, identity document, qualification certificates, other documents you choose to upload To verify trainers before they can be listed or booked, and to set the platform price
Bookings Session type (at home or online), date and time, status, the price at confirmation, cancellation and reschedule history To run the booking, keep both sides informed and resolve disputes
Session address The address a client gives for an at-home session To let the trainer reach the session. It is visible only to the client and the trainer, and only after the booking is confirmed
Availability A trainer's weekly availability and time off To offer bookable slots
Messages Messages between a client and a trainer inside the App To let you communicate about sessions
Reviews Star rating, written review, the booking it relates to To show other clients verified feedback. Trainers see a short display name, never your email
Progress Weight entries by date and an optional note that you choose to log To show you your own trend. Progress entries are visible only to you
Trainer notes Private notes a trainer keeps about a client To help the trainer plan sessions. Notes are visible only to the trainer who wrote them
Support and reports Support cases, reports about a person, booking, message or review, and the details you provide To handle your request, keep the platform safe and meet our legal obligations
Notifications Your notification preferences and the notifications we send you in the App To tell you about booking changes, messages and reminders
Device activity Step counts from your phone's activity sensors, only if you turn this on and grant the system permission To show your daily activity in the App. We do not sell or share it
Technical App version, device type and operating system version, error reports, access logs kept by our hosting provider To keep the App secure and working
Waitlist (Site) Email address, whether you are a client or trainer, optional city To tell you when BRUTAL opens in your area

We do not collect precise GPS location in the background, contacts, photos other than the ones you upload, or payment card details (see section 5).

3. How we use data

We use the data above to provide the App and the Site, verify trainers, run bookings, send service notifications, keep the platform safe, answer support requests, comply with law and improve the App. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Legal basis

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (for example when you upload documents, log progress or enable device activity); the performance of our agreement with you (running your account and bookings); our legitimate interests in keeping the platform safe and improving it; and our legal obligations.

5. Who we share data with

  • Trainers and clients you book with see what they need for the session: display name, profile, booking details and, once a booking is confirmed, the session address.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure provider, Supabase, stores the App's data (database, authentication, file storage) on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Email delivery providers send verification codes and service emails.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy; BRUTAL never sees your card or UPI details. Until launch the App charges in test mode only, so no real money moves.
  • An AI coach feature is on our roadmap. If we launch it, we will say what data it uses and which provider processes it before you can enable it.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, and professional advisers under confidentiality.

We do not include advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs in the App.

6. International transfers

Our infrastructure provider may store data on servers outside India. Where it does, we rely on the provider's contractual safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account is active. Bookings, reviews, support cases and reports are kept as long as needed to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Trainer verification documents are kept while the trainer is active on the platform and for the period the law requires afterwards. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent. In the App you can:

  • edit your profile and preferences in Settings;
  • export your data (Settings, Privacy, Request data export);
  • delete your account (Settings, Privacy, Delete account), or follow the steps on our account deletion page;
  • turn notifications and device activity on or off.

For anything else, write to [privacy contact email]. We respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is controlled row by row so a client cannot read another client's data and a trainer cannot read another trainer's. Verification documents, support records, progress entries and private notes are the most restricted data in the App. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Children

The App is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
